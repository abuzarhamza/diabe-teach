# 02 - Study Methodology & RCT Design

## Ethical Approval & Trial Protocol

- **Institutional Review Board:** Research and Ethics Committee at Dubai Medical College for Girls (REC Approval No: `IBR-2022-23-F-17`).
- **Declaration of Helsinki:** Study conformed to ethical principles outlined in the 1964 Declaration of Helsinki and subsequent amendments.
- **Informed Consent:** Explicit written consent was secured from all participants prior to study inclusion, with guaranteed confidentiality.

---

## Study Population & Eligibility Criteria

- **Target Population:** Preclinical undergraduate medical students (Years 1 to 3) enrolled at Dubai Medical College for Girls (Muhaisanah, Dubai, UAE).
- **Curricular Context:** Students were enrolled just prior to beginning their formal systemic endocrinology module. This ensured baseline naive knowledge regarding detailed diabetes pathophysiology and pharmacotherapy.
- **Cohort Size:** 64 total students were in this preclinical stage, of which 56 volunteered and met full eligibility criteria.

### Inclusion Criteria
1. Currently enrolled in the preclinical stage (Years 1 to 3) of the undergraduate medical curriculum.
2. Provided written informed consent.
3. Had not previously studied diabetes mellitus in depth.

### Exclusion Criteria
1. Participants who dropped out during the 2-hour session.
2. Self-reported deep prior clinical or scientific study of diabetes mellitus.
3. Statistical outliers on the pretest (scores $> 2$ standard deviations above the mean) to avoid confounding bias from atypical baseline mastery.

---

## Power Analysis & Sample Size

- **Target Effect Size:** Medium to large effect size ($d = 0.8$).
- **Significance Level ($\alpha$):** $0.05$.
- **Statistical Power ($1 - \beta$):** $0.80$ (80%).
- **Minimum Required Sample Size:** 52 participants ($26$ per arm).
- **Actual Enrolled Sample Size:** 56 participants ($28$ per arm), satisfying and exceeding power requirements.

---

## Randomization Procedure

- **Method:** True computer-generated simple randomization via [Randomizer.org](https://www.randomizer.org).
- **Allocation:** Each student was assigned a unique ID number. The automated algorithm distributed students into two balanced parallel arms (1:1 allocation ratio):
  - **Intervention Arm ($n = 28$):** Diabe-teach board game learning.
  - **Control Arm ($n = 28$):** Structured independent self-study.

---

## Standardized Session Timeline (120 Minutes)

The study was executed in a tightly controlled 2-hour window to maintain protocol fidelity across cohorts:

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Total Duration: 120 Minutes                                                   |
+---------------------+-------------------------------+-------------------------+
| Phase               | Duration                      | Description             |
+---------------------+-------------------------------+-------------------------+
| 1. Pretest          | 15 min                        | 10 MCQs (baseline)      |
| 2. Intervention     | 75 min                        | Board game vs Self-study|
|    - Orientation    |   (15 min)                    | Rules + trial round     |
|    - Round 1        |   (30 min)                    | 20m play + 10m review   |
|    - Round 2        |   (30 min)                    | Shuffled board repeat   |
| 3. Post-test        | 15 min                        | Identical 10 MCQs       |
| 4. Feedback Survey  | 15 min                        | 9 Likert survey items   |
+---------------------+-------------------------------+-------------------------+
```

### 1. Pretest (15 Minutes)
- Administered to all 56 participants prior to material distribution.
- Composed of 10 clinical multiple-choice questions (MCQs) mapped to Bloom's taxonomy cognitive levels 2 (Comprehension) and 3 (Application).
- Included a 5th response option: *"I don't know"* alongside 1 correct answer and 3 plausible distractors.

### 2. Intervention Phase (75 Minutes)
Both groups received **identical content coverage** (pathophysiology, clinical manifestations, laboratory diagnostics, and pharmacologic management of diabetes mellitus).

#### Intervention Group (Diabe-teach Board Game)
- **Orientation & Trial Round (15 minutes):** An instructor walked students through the card deck, adjacency rules, and sample chains. Students practiced building and explaining sample chains to ensure complete familiarity with mechanics.
- **Game Round 1 (30 minutes):** Cards were shuffled and set up in a fresh 7×7 grid.
  - *20 minutes:* Independent/group card examination, chain mapping, recording on paper.
  - *10 minutes:* Chain verbalization, explanation of medical rationale to peers, instructor review and consensus validation.
- **Game Round 2 (30 minutes):** Full deck reshuffle, new 7×7 grid layout, repeating the 20 min mapping + 10 min presentation and scoring cycle.

#### Control Group (Structured Self-Study)
- Hosted in a quiet, proctored classroom environment.
- Provided with printed handouts and identical digital documentation covering the exact same subject domains as the 49 cards.
- Conducted strictly as individual study (no verbal collaboration or peer discussions permitted).

### 3. Post-Test (15 Minutes)
- Administered immediately following the completion of the 75-minute learning period.
- Used the identical 10-question instrument to evaluate immediate recall and short-term knowledge retention.

### 4. Perception & Feedback Survey (15 Minutes)
- Administered anonymously via online survey to the intervention cohort.
- Validated 9-item Likert scale (1 = Highly Disagree to 5 = Highly Agree).
- Measured ease of play, cognitive engagement, stress levels, peer communication, and perceived utility.
