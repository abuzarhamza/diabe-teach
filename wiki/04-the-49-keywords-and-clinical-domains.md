# 04 - The 49 Keywords & Clinical Domains

Appendix 1 of the publication defines the complete set of 49 keywords chosen by endocrine medical educators. These terms encompass the fundamental pillars of undergraduate diabetes curricula: pathophysiology, pharmacology, diagnostics, clinical presentation, acute and chronic complications, and clinical management.

---

## Complete Catalog of 49 Terms

Below is the verified alphabetical master list of cards included in the Diabe-teach deck:

| # | Keyword | Clinical Domain | Primary Medical Concept |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **ACE Inhibitors** | Pharmacology & Nephroprotection | Renoprotective antihypertensive; prevents progression of diabetic nephropathy. |
| 2 | **Adiponectin** | Endocrine / Adipokine Biochemistry | Anti-inflammatory, insulin-sensitizing hormone secreted by adipose tissue (decreased in obesity/T2DM). |
| 3 | **Amylin** | Pancreatic Endocrinology | Co-secreted with insulin by beta cells; inhibits postprandial glucagon secretion and slows gastric emptying. |
| 4 | **Autoimmune** | Pathogenesis & Etiology | Autoantibody-mediated destruction of pancreatic islet beta cells (Type 1 DM, LADA). |
| 5 | **Beta cells** | Cellular Anatomy & Histology | Endocrine cells in pancreatic islets of Langerhans that synthesize and secrete insulin and amylin. |
| 6 | **C-Peptide** | Laboratory Diagnostics | Cleaved connecting peptide from proinsulin; biomarker of endogenous insulin reserve unaffected by exogenous insulin. |
| 7 | **Children** | Epidemiology & Demographics | Pediatric diabetes considerations (classic presentation of Type 1 DM, neonatal diabetes, rising pediatric T2DM). |
| 8 | **Diet** | Non-Pharmacologic Management | Medical nutrition therapy, macronutrient distribution, carbohydrate counting, caloric restriction. |
| 9 | **Dys-lipidaemia** | Metabolic Comorbidities | Atherogenic lipid triad in insulin resistance: high triglycerides, low HDL, elevated small dense LDL particles. |
| 10 | **Fasting** | Diagnostic Criteria | Fasting plasma glucose ($\ge 126\text{ mg/dL}$ or $7.0\text{ mmol/L}$) after an 8-hour fast for formal DM diagnosis. |
| 11 | **Gastro-paresis** | Autonomic Neuropathy Complication | Delayed gastric emptying due to vagus nerve damage in long-standing diabetic autonomic neuropathy. |
| 12 | **Genetic** | Etiology & Susceptibility | Polygenic risk alleles (HLA-DR3/DR4 in T1DM) and monogenic mutations (HNF1A, GCK in MODY). |
| 13 | **Glipizide** | Pharmacotherapy | Second-generation sulfonylurea; binds SUR1 receptor on beta cells stimulating insulin secretion. |
| 14 | **Glucagon** | Pancreatic Counter-Regulatory Hormone | Secreted by alpha cells; stimulates hepatic glycogenolysis and gluconeogenesis, elevating blood glucose. |
| 15 | **Glucometer** | Self-Monitoring Technology | Point-of-care capillary blood glucose monitoring device used for ambulatory self-titration and safety. |
| 16 | **Glucose** | Fundamental Biochemistry | Primary circulating hexose monosaccharide and cellular fuel substrate whose homeostasis is disrupted. |
| 17 | **Glucosuria** | Renal Pathophysiology | Presence of glucose in urine when plasma glucose exceeds renal absorptive threshold ($\approx 180\text{ mg/dL}$). |
| 18 | **Glycemic index** | Nutritional Science | Numerical scale ranking carbohydrate foods based on the velocity and extent of postprandial glycemic rise. |
| 19 | **GTT** | Diagnostic Testing | Oral Glucose Tolerance Test; standard diagnostic challenge test (evaluating 2-hour post 75g glucose load). |
| 20 | **HbA1c** | Long-Term Biomarker | Glycated hemoglobin; reflects average glycemic exposure over previous 8–12 weeks ($\ge 6.5\%$ diagnostic). |
| 21 | **HHS** | Acute Emergency Complication | Hyperosmolar Hyperglycemic State; severe hyperglycemia ($>600\text{ mg/dL}$), hyperosmolality, absence of ketoacidosis in T2DM. |
| 22 | **Hypertension** | Micro/Macrovascular Comorbidity | Co-morbid cardiovascular risk factor multiplying accelerated atherosclerosis, stroke, and nephropathy risk. |
| 23 | **Hyperglycaemia** | Hallmark Clinical Sign | Elevated blood glucose; core pathophysiological driver of osmotic diuresis and glucotoxicity. |
| 24 | **Hypo-glycemia** | Acute Adverse Complication | Dangerously low blood glucose ($<70\text{ mg/dL}$), neuroglycopenic and autonomic crisis, often iatrogenic from insulin/SU. |
| 25 | **Insulin** | Central Hormone / Biologic Agent | Anabolic peptide hormone facilitating cellular glucose uptake via GLUT4, suppressing lipolysis and gluconeogenesis. |
| 26 | **Islet Cells** | Pancreatic Anatomy | Islets of Langerhans containing $\alpha, \beta, \delta,$ and $PP$ cells regulating metabolic homeostasis. |
| 27 | **Keto-acidosis** | Acute Metabolic Emergency | Diabetic Ketoacidosis (DKA); profound insulin deficiency leading to uninhibited lipolysis, ketogenesis, and metabolic acidosis. |
| 28 | **LADA** | Disease Classification / Subtype | Latent Autoimmune Diabetes in Adults; slow-onset autoimmune diabetes presenting in adulthood often misdiagnosed as T2DM. |
| 29 | **Lipo-hypertrophy** | Injection Complication | Localized subcutaneous fibrofatty lump at repeated needle injection sites, causing unpredictable insulin absorption. |
| 30 | **Metformin** | First-line Oral Pharmacotherapy | Biguanide; activates AMPK, decreases hepatic gluconeogenesis, enhances peripheral insulin sensitivity. |
| 31 | **Microalbuminuria** | Microvascular Screening Marker | Urinary albumin excretion (30–300 mg/24h or albumin-to-creatinine ratio); early indicator of diabetic nephropathy. |
| 32 | **MODY** | Genetic Monogenic Subtype | Maturity-Onset Diabetes of the Young; autosomal dominant monogenic non-insulin-dependent diabetes of young onset. |
| 33 | **Neuropathy** | Microvascular Complication | Distal symmetric polyneuropathy and autonomic neuropathy secondary to microvascular ischemic and sorbitol damage. |
| 34 | **Pancreas** | Organ Anatomy & Physiology | Dual exocrine/endocrine organ situated in retroperitoneum responsible for digestive enzymes and endocrine islets. |
| 35 | **Podiatrist** | Multidisciplinary Care Provider | Foot health specialist managing high-risk diabetic foot ulcer prevention, biomechanical debridement, and footwear. |
| 36 | **Polydipsia** | Classic Cardinal Symptom | Excessive thirst triggered by hypothalamic osmoreceptor stimulation due to serum hyperosmolality from hyperglycemia. |
| 37 | **Polyphagia** | Classic Cardinal Symptom | Persistent hunger resulting from cellular energy starvation due to impaired cellular glucose transport. |
| 38 | **Polyuria** | Classic Cardinal Symptom | Excessive urination caused by osmotic diuresis when tubular glucose exceeds renal resorptive threshold. |
| 39 | **Pregnancy** | Special Clinical Setting | Gestational diabetes mellitus (GDM) and pre-gestational diabetes requiring intensive glycemic management to prevent fetal macrosomia. |
| 40 | **Retinopathy** | Microvascular Complication | Microaneurysms, hemorrhages, hard exudates, and neovascularization of the retina leading to adult-onset blindness. |
| 41 | **Screening** | Preventive Public Health | Routine targeted testing in high-risk asymptomatic adults to detect prediabetes and early diabetes. |
| 42 | **Smoking** | Modifiable Lifestyle Risk Factor | Tobacco smoke accelerates endothelial dysfunction and doubles the risk of diabetic macrovascular complications. |
| 43 | **Sulfonyl-ureas** | Oral Pharmacotherapy Class | Class of insulin secretagogues (e.g., glipizide, glimepiride) closing ATP-sensitive potassium channels. |
| 44 | **Thiazolidine-diones** | Oral Pharmacotherapy Class | PPAR-$\gamma$ agonists (pioglitazone) that improve insulin sensitivity in adipose, muscle, and liver tissues. |
| 45 | **Transplant** | Advanced Surgical Therapy | Whole-organ pancreas transplantation or islet cell transplantation for refractory brittle Type 1 DM or ESRD. |
| 46 | **Ulcer** | Chronic Complication / Diabetic Foot | Complex chronic neuropathic/neuro-ischemic foot wound predisposing to osteomyelitis and amputation. |
| 47 | **Venous** | Clinical Laboratory / Anatomy | Venous plasma blood samples (as opposed to capillary whole blood) required for formal diagnostic confirmation. |
| 48 | **Weight** | Anthropometric Risk Metric | Body weight / BMI; central visceral adiposity is the strongest driver of insulin resistance and T2DM. |
| 49 | **Weight loss** | Dual Clinical Significance | (1) Presenting symptom of untreated T1DM/DKA catabolism; (2) Therapeutic target in overweight T2DM patients. |
