How to play

Read while you wait
  1. Each round you get a 7×7 board of diabetes keywords, shuffled fresh.
  2. Tap keywords that touch each other: sideways, up/down or diagonally. You can't use a keyword twice.
  3. Explain each link in your own words, then tell the story of the whole chain.
  4. Submit as many chains as you like before the timer runs out.
  5. In peer review, approve or challenge classmates' chains. Your teacher has the final say.
  6. Your longest approved chain ranks you, then how many chains were approved.

Example chain

Autoimmune → Beta cells → Insulin → Hyperglycemia

An autoimmune attack destroys the pancreatic beta cells, so the body can't make enough insulin, and blood glucose rises into hyperglycemia.

Each keyword touches the next one, and every link has a reason. A chain like this would be approved.
